I’m Ahmad Alhour, a computer scientist by education and a software engineer by trade, originally from 🇯🇴 Amman, Jordan, and currently living in 🇩🇪 Munich, Germany.

I like following systems all the way down: from slick product interfaces and reliable backends to infrastructure, networks and storage.

Previously, I led product engineering at [Shopify](https://shop.app/), product & data engineering at [TrustYou](https://www.trustyou.com/), built global data infrastructure at [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/), and co-founded [Vamu](https://vamu.io/), a cybersecurity startup (since exited).

This is my digital spot where I [write about](/archives/) software, books, philosophy, and whatever else I'm currently curious about. I also publish hacks, tinkers, and [side projects](/projects/) I've built over the years... mostly for fun, and rarely for profit.

I build [interactive animations](/animations/) when words need a moving picture, publish some of my [talks](/talks/), and sometimes lose myself in [mathematical puzzles](https://projecteuler.net/).

When I’m not building or debugging things, I’m usually reading something. Recent favorites include: [*The Beginning of Infinity*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/10483171-the-beginning-of-infinity), [*The Optimistic Child*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7489.The_Optimistic_Child), and [*What If?*](www.goodreads.com/book/show/21413662-what-if)... which is probably a fair summary of my reading taste: scientific explanation, parenting, and ridiculous hypotheticals! You can find more of that trail on my [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/aalhour).
